Nightfall Reliquary

A single-file first-person horror roguelike RPG

Descend through a procedurally corrupted reliquary, steal the Saint of Ash from the fifth depth, and survive long enough to remember your name. Every run changes its layout, loot, shrines, monsters, and notes. Death is permanent; relic shards persist.

ControlsWASD move · Mouse turn · Click attack · E interact · R tonic · 1/2 weapon · Tab inventory · Esc pause.
Run StructureFive depths, procedural rooms, RPG leveling, relic builds, sanity pressure, permadeath, and persistent legacy shards.
AudioProcedural WebAudio drones, footsteps, impacts, whispers, and alert stingers. Browser audio starts after input.
Release ConstraintNo CDN, no external art, no external scripts. This page is the game.
