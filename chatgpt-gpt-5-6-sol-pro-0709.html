Field doctrine

How to survive

Move deliberately. Sound draws the congregation. Sprinting is fast, loud, and exhausting. Evading grants a brief moment of safety.

Manage the cylinder. Reload before a room, not inside one. A melee strike costs no ammunition and can interrupt weaker creatures.

Protect your resolve. Nearby horrors drain it. Low resolve bends vision, lies through subtitles, and creates apparitions that cannot be killed.

Every descent mutates. Rooms, enemies, relics, lore, and growth choices are generated from the run seed. Death erases the run; records remain.
