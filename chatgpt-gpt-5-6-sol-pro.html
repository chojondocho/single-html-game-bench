Survival doctrine

Collect every seal before the descent opens. Darkness and certain creatures erode Mind. Low Mind creates false threats and weakens recovery. Listen before entering a room; sprinting is loud. Lantern rites stagger nearby horrors, but their charge is slow.

Gamepad: left/right sticks move/look, RT attack, LT lantern, A interact, LB sprint, Y cycle, D-pad items, Start pause. Touch devices receive virtual movement, look, attack, lantern, and interact controls.
